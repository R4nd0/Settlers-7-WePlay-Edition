# Integration symbols

These symbols identify existing integrations in the launcher. They are not
WePlay's own logo or a claim of endorsement. The application embeds them and
does not download images at runtime.

- `discord.svg`: unchanged Blurple symbol from Discord's
  [official branding page](https://discord.com/branding),
  [original SVG](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6257adef93867e50d84d30e2/66e3d80db9971f10a9757c99_Symbol.svg).
  `discord.png` is a proportional raster rendering of that SVG, with its shape
  and colors retained. Discord owns its marks; the symbol is shown alongside
  the Discord name to identify the publishing integration.
- `easytier.png`: unchanged `easytier-gui/src-tauri/icons/icon.png` from the
  pinned [EasyTier source](https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier) already used
  for WePlay's bundled transport. EasyTier is distributed under LGPL-3.0;
  its corresponding-source and license distribution are retained by packaging.

The four larger mode symbols are drawn by `ModeSymbol` in the launcher source.
They use distinct shapes for a player, connected PCs, a locked globe and a globe
with players. Color is an additional cue. Brand symbols keep their original
proportions and colors and have adjacent readable labels.
